01 · THE ACADEMIC

Mathematics as a conversation.

My research lies in Combinatorial Commutative Algebra, particularly in understanding how combinatorial structures influence the algebraic and homological properties of associated ideals.

TheTrefoil brings together my mathematical research, publications, teaching materials and resources for students, fellow researchers and anyone curious about mathematics.

My academic training includes a doctorate from the Central University of Jammu and a D. S. Kothari postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Kashmir.
