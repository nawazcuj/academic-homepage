01 / THE ACADEMIC

Mathematics as a conversation.

My research lies in Combinatorial Commutative Algebra, particularly in understanding how combinatorial structures influence the algebraic and homological properties of associated ideals.

TheTrefoil brings together my mathematical research, publications, teaching materials and resources for students, fellow researchers and anyone curious about mathematics.

I am Shahnawaz Ahmad Rather, an Assistant Professor of Mathematics specializing in Combinatorial Commutative Algebra. My research focuses on monomial ideals, their connections with graph theory and combinatorics, and the study of algebraic invariants such as minimal graded free resolutions, graded Betti numbers, projective dimension, and regularity.

Through TheTrefoil, I share mathematical ideas, lecture notes, and educational resources to encourage curiosity, rigorous thinking, and deeper understanding.
